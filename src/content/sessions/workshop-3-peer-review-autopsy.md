A written autopsy is easy to get right in your own head and wrong on the
page — the connections feel obvious to the person who lived them and often
aren't obvious to a reader starting cold. This is the one workshop in the
semester built entirely around outside eyes.

## Before the workshop

Bring a complete draft, even a rough one. A half-finished draft is more useful
here than a polished paragraph or two — reviewers need to see the whole shape
of the argument.

## In the workshop

Paired review, swapped twice. Each reader is asked one specific question:
where does this draft's interpretation go further than the evidence it's
presented? Not "is this well written" — specifically, where is the leap.

## Afterwards

Revise before the Meaning Autopsy is due at the end of week 7. The most common
change students make after this session is cutting a paragraph that was doing
more interpreting than describing.
