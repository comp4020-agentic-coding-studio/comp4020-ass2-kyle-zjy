The Pattern Diary is the running thread underneath the whole semester, and
this workshop is where it starts. The habit is simple to describe and
surprisingly easy to do badly: notice something — a repeated number, a run of
bad luck, a dream that lined up with the next day — and write down what
happened *before* you write down what you think it means. Most people's first
instinct is to skip straight to the interpretation. The diary asks you to
slow that down by one step.

## Before the workshop

Bring whatever you're going to actually use for twelve weeks. A dedicated
notebook works. So does a notes app. What doesn't work is a format you'll
abandon by week 4 — the exercise depends on volume.

## In the workshop

A short demonstration of a strong entry versus a weak one, then time to write
your first real entry about something from the last few days. Wren reads a
handful anonymously and the group discusses, out loud, where the noticing
stopped and the interpreting started.

## Afterwards

Keep going. The diary itself isn't marked week to week — it feeds the Pattern
Diary assessment directly, so an entry written honestly now is easier to use
later than one written to look good in retrospect.
