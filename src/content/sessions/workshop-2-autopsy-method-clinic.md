Taking a coincidence apart is a specific skill, and it's easy to do it badly
in two opposite directions: explaining it away too fast ("obviously
confirmation bias") without engaging what actually made it feel striking, or
taking it too seriously without ever checking the boring explanation first.
This workshop is a rehearsal for the Meaning Autopsy, using material you've
already collected.

## Before the workshop

Bring the strongest candidate from your Pattern Diary so far — the entry you'd
be most tempted to call "too weird to be a coincidence."

## In the workshop

Small groups take turns presenting one diary entry each and applying a short
checklist: what was noticed, what it was linked to, whether the link survived
contact with what actually happened, and which of the frameworks from weeks 1
to 4 best explains the noticing itself. The group's job is to push on the
weakest point in each other's account, not to agree with it.

## Afterwards

Choose which entry you'll autopsy properly and start reading around the
framework you'll need — dream theory, probability, or the omen-reading
pattern from week 3 are all fair game depending on what you picked.
