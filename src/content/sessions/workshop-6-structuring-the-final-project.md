Every earlier assessment in the semester practises one piece of the method:
noticing (Pattern Diary), taking a single case apart (Meaning Autopsy), and
building the machinery behind a reading (Build an Oracle). The final project
asks you to run the whole method on the moment in your own life that felt most
like fate, and to write both halves honestly — what it felt like, and what
you can now say about why.

## Before the workshop

Choose the moment. It doesn't need to be dramatic — a smaller coincidence
that's stayed with you is easier to write about honestly than one you've
already turned into a polished story.

## In the workshop

Wren and Sanjay run a joint structuring session: a short framework for
splitting the piece into what you believed at the time and what you believe
now, followed by one-on-one time to plan the specific frameworks from the
semester you'll bring to bear on your own case.

## Afterwards

Write. The most common failure mode in past final projects is skipping
straight to the tidy explanation and losing the honest first half — what it
actually felt like, before you knew the trick.
