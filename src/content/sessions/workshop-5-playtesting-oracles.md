The gap between what a designer intends and what a user actually experiences
is the whole subject of this course, applied to something you built yourself.
This workshop puts that gap in front of you directly, a fortnight before Build
an Oracle is due.

## Before the workshop

Have a version of your oracle that works well enough to hand to a stranger
without a live tutorial. Rough is fine. Silent is not — you need to be able to
step back and watch.

## In the workshop

Paired playtesting: hand your oracle to a partner, say nothing, and watch what
they do with it. Swap. Afterwards, each pair compares notes on where the
partner's reading of a result matched the designer's intent and where it
diverged — often in the direction of reading in more meaning than was built
in.

## Afterwards

Adjust the mechanism disclosure if playtesting showed people missing it, and
finish the build. What you watched a stranger do with your oracle is worth a
sentence or two in the reflection you submit alongside it.
